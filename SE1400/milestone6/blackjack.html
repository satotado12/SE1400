How to Play

Blackjack is a fast-paced game combining skill and luck. The goal is to get a hand total as close to 21 as possible without busting, while beating the dealer's hand. Each round begins with players placing their bets, and both the player and dealer are dealt two cards — the player’s cards are usually face up while the dealer has one face up and one face down.

Players can Hit to take another card or Stand with their current total. The dealer must hit on 16 and stand on 17 in most versions.

Card Values

  • Number cards (2–10) = face value
  • Face cards (Jack, Queen, King) = 10 points
  • Ace = 1 or 11 (whichever benefits the hand)

Basic Strategy Tips

  • Always hit on 8 or less
  • Stand on 17 or higher
  • Split Aces and 8s
  • Never take insurance
  • Double down on 11 when the dealer shows a low card
