How to Play Roulette

Roulette is a classic casino game where a ball is spun around a wheel with numbered pockets. Players bet on where the ball will land.

You can bet on single numbers, red/black, odd/even, dozens, columns, and many other combinations.

Types of Bets

  • Inside Bets: Single number, split, street, corner
  • Outside Bets: Red/Black, Odd/Even, High/Low, Dozens, Columns

Roulette is a game of pure chance. European Roulette (single zero) gives better odds than American Roulette (double zero).
