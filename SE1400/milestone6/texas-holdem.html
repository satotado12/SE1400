How to Play Texas Hold'Em

Texas Hold'Em is a community card poker game. Each player is dealt two private hole cards, and five community cards are dealt face up on the table.

There are four betting rounds: Pre-Flop, Flop, Turn, and River. The player with the best five-card hand wins the pot.

Basic Strategy Tips

  • Play tight in early position and wider in late position
  • Premium starting hands: AA, KK, QQ, AK, AQ
  • Position is very important — act last when possible
  • Pay attention to bet sizing and opponent tendencies
